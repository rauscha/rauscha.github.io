Almagest

A chord instrument for your phone, drawn as a star chart of the circle of fifths. Chords are stars and progressions are constellations: play a chord and the ones likely to follow brighten, counted from real music, while shared notes hold and you watch the voices move. Nine classic constellations — ii–V–I to Pachelbel to the twelve-bar blues — are drawn on the chart for you to trace. Made for an Android phone held sideways, with MIDI out over USB if you’d like it to play your synths instead.

Shadowbox

Interactive lessons on the statistics behind fitting data. Walks through simulated data, then a maternal-fetal medicine data set, to show you how least squares, covariance, PCA, k-means, etc. work. Available lessons will continue to expand into more statistical and machine learning methods over time.

Fetal Doppler Cascade

An interactive teaching tool for the Doppler changes of placental insufficiency. Drag a single slider from a healthy fetus toward critical and watch the umbilical-artery, middle-cerebral-artery, and ductus-venosus waveforms deteriorate in the order they actually do — rising UA resistance, then MCA brain-sparing, then the DV a-wave reversing last. Five checkpoint stops with a clinical readout at each. Pulled from a maternal-fetal-medicine lecture on pulse-wave Doppler.

Intrauterine Transfusion Calculator

A phone-first clinical tool for maternal-fetal medicine. From the current fetal weight and hematocrit it calculates red-cell and platelet transfusion volumes and per-kilogram procedure medication doses, with a normative fetal hemoglobin reference by gestational age. Formulas from Mandelbrot 1988, verified to five decimals against the source spreadsheet. Decision support only — verify every value before use.

Sleep App

A web app built around one thing: sleep. It layers incommensurate loops and ambient soundscapes with sleep stories and meditations, all running on your phone, with no accounts required and no backend. In active development.

Critter Radar

A phone-friendly companion for Dreamlight Valley made for my wife. Tap to log each daily feeding, watch your tallies climb toward a new companion, and check which critters are out right now based on the in-game clock and weather. Progress is saved on your device, with a one-tap export for backups, and it installs to your home screen like a native app.

Work in progress

A browser playground for the Mutable Instruments Braids macro-oscillator — Émilie Gillet’s open-source synthesizer DSP, compiled to WebAssembly and run live in the page. Scope, 4 bar staff, the synth, and an explanation of what each variable changes on a given model. MIDI import/export and in/out incoming.
